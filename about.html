Our Story

About Urban Hairplaza

Born in Simraha, Araria. Built on community, quality, and passion.

✂️
Founded with Purpose

Where Tradition Meets Modern Craft

Urban Hairplaza was born from a vision to bring premium barbershop quality to the people of Simraha, Araria. We believe that everyone deserves to look and feel their best, without having to travel far from home.

Our master barbers bring years of expertise in both classic and modern grooming techniques. We combine traditional barbershop culture with the latest trends and technology — including AI-powered hairstyle visualization.

Community is at the heart of everything we do. From our in-shop experience to our home visit service, we strive to make premium grooming accessible to all.
